# Editing your Ginger & Crumb website

Go to **your website address + /admin** (for example gingerandcrumb.ca/admin) and log in with GitHub.

| To change… | Open… |
|---|---|
| Spots left each week | Weekly availability |
| Prices, menu items, photos | Menu & prices |
| Holiday box, Valentine's drop, Easter special | Holiday / seasonal special |
| Order deadlines | Order deadlines |
| Top banner, your name, intro text, pickup times | Business details & page text |
| Questions | FAQ |

Then click **Publish → Publish now**. Your site updates in about a minute.

**Weekly habit (2 minutes):** update "Spots left" for each week, delete last week, add a new week at the bottom, and update "Claimed so far" on any special.

**Tips**
- Spots left 0 shows "Fully booked". 1–3 shows "Only X spots left". 4 or more shows "Booking now".
- Turn off "Available" on a menu item to mark it unavailable. It also disappears from the order form.
- Photos: square photos look best on the menu. Taken in window light, from above or at a 45° angle.
- When a special sells out (Claimed = Total), the site shows "Sold out" and removes it from the order form on its own.
